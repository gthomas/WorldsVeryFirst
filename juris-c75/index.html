    The original Juris C-75 "The Plaintiff": a 2 in chambered mahogany body with a maple cap, on the geometric outline with heavy bevels on the top and back edges. Set mahogany neck at 4°, 24.75 in scale, '59 roundback profile, 3+3 Juris headstock with a riveted asset-tag badge.

    Two surface-mount T-Armond style pickups in rings, a floating bridge on a flat-bottom rosewood base, and the geometric trapeze tailpiece with its diamond. Two volume and two tone knobs in the treble lower bout, a toggle in the bass horn, and a small pickguard on the treble side.
