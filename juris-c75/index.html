    Construction

    The original Juris C-75 "The Plaintiff": a 2 in chambered mahogany body with a maple cap, on the geometric outline with heavy bevels on the top and back edges. Set mahogany neck at 4° with an ebony board, 24.75 in scale, '59 roundback profile; 3+3 Juris headstock with waisted sides and a gabled top, truss rod adjusted through a slot above the nut, and a riveted asset-tag badge.

    Two surface-mount T-Armond style pickups over humbucker-size routes, a floating bridge on a flat-bottom rosewood base, and the geometric trapeze tailpiece with its diamond. Volume and tone in the treble lower bout with a Gretsch-style 3-way tone switch just left of them, a pickup toggle in the bass horn, and a small pickguard on the treble side.
