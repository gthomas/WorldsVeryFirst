    Construction

    One- or two-piece solid body, 1-3/4 in, on the C-75 outline with its bevelled edges: a continuous band follows the edge all the way round, falling about 11 mm to the outline on the top and the back, and leaving a square strip round the side. Machined in two setups on a 3-axis router (top, then flipped for the back), with a 1/4 in ball-nose for the bevels.

    Bolt-on 25.5 in neck in a standard Fender-pattern pocket meeting the body at the 16th fret: a custom maple neck (21 frets, 12 in radius, ebony board) with the C-75's 13° tilt-back Juris headstock and volute, so no string tree is needed; truss rod adjusts through a slot above the nut; Grover 3+3. Classic T layout with no pickguard: vintage T bridge pickup on its baseplate in a 3-saddle plate with strings through the body, a chrome-covered T neck pickup screwed to the body on springs, and a 5-1/2 in control plate with 3-way switch, volume and tone, set parallel to the treble bevel. Cup jack on the square treble tail edge.
