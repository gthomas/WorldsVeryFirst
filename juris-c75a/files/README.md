# Juris C-75A: faceted acoustic archtop on the C-75 outline

Parametric model. Units are mm. Axes: x+ toward the treble side, y+ toward the headstock (y = 0 at the tail), z+ up (z = 0 at the back edge).

## Files
- `juris_c75a.glb`: full assembly (open it in Blender, Fusion or any glTF viewer)
- `top.stl`, `back.stl`, `rim.stl`, `neck.stl`, `fingerboard.stl`, `bridge.stl`: watertight solids for CAM
- `juris_c75a_templates.dxf`: 2D templates on separate layers. OUTLINE, RIM_INNER (7 mm wall), LINING_INNER (12 mm ledge), TOP_PLATEAU, TOP_BEAM, TOP_RIB, TOP_FOLDS, SOUNDHOLES, BACK_PLATEAU, BACK_FOLDS, CENTERLINE, BRIDGE_LINE
- `report.json`: derived numbers (bridge height, clearances, weight estimate, air volume)
- `fea.py` and `fea.json`: static finite-element check of the top under string load (quadratic tets, orthotropic quartersawn spruce, top fixed where it is glued to the rim and neck block)
- `build.py` and `outline.py`: the generator. Edit `P` in build.py and run `python build.py` (needs manifold3d, trimesh, mapbox_earcut, ezdxf, numpy)

## How the geometry is built
- **Outline:** traced from Juris_C_75_Body_2D.pdf and calibrated on its 18.000 and 13.500 dimensions. The lower bout is symmetrised, and the corners are filleted (12 to 19 mm, sized for a 12 mm end mill).
- **Top:** made of planes only, symmetric about the centreline. A flat hexagonal bridge plateau (141 x 155 mm, wide enough for the 135 mm Gretsch base) sits 14 mm above the edge inside an eight-sided arch zone inset 22 mm from the outline; 14 triangular facets run from the plateau to that octagon, and outside it the top is a flat skirt at edge level that sits on the rim and glue ledges. Falls taper 3.8 to 3.2 mm, the plateau is 6 mm thick, a 5 x 8 mm ring beam runs under the plateau edge (the base's feet land on it), and two 6 x 9 mm tone bars with 35 mm scalloped ends run lengthwise under the plateau's long sides, and a 6 x 7 mm cross bar runs from the neck block's lower face out to the bass upper bout, tapering over its last 35 mm and stopping 2 mm short of the glue ledge (all on DXF layer TOP_BARS). The two soundholes are mirror images in the side facets beside the bridge (about 18 x 48 mm each), outboard of the bars. The pickup lead drops through a 6 mm hole under the fingerboard extension.
- **Back:** quartersawn bigleaf maple (top is quartersawn Sitka spruce). A single centre ridge 7 mm deep: two long flat panels meeting along the centreline over a 152 mm wide hexagonal footprint with pointed ends, and a flat border outside it that sits on the glue ledge. 3.2 mm thick throughout, unbraced; the ridge and footprint fold lines act as stiffeners (DXF layer BACK_FOLDS).
- **Rim:** routed from one 12/4 mahogany blank, finished 57.1 mm (2.25") tall for a 2.5" body at the edge. It has 7 mm walls, 12 x 8 mm glue ledges routed top and bottom, solid neck and tail blocks, a neck mortise, an endpin bore and the control window.
- **Edges:** 3.5 mm 45-degree chamfer around the top and back edges, cut after glue-up through the plate and into the rim.
- **Heel and volute:** the '59 round carve runs all the way into the heel, which sweeps down to finish flush with the back edge on the body face. The heel is 53 mm wide, 1.5 mm inside the body's sides, and lands on a square, unchamfered end face. Behind the nut the round neck section morphs into the square headstock section with no step; the volute loft forms the headstock itself up to the first tuners, so there is no seam at the base. The edge chamfer fades out over the last 28 mm before the neck end face.
- **Neck tongue:** the neck continues as a tongue under the fingerboard extension, filling the space between the top and the board over the neck block (to y = 392 mm) and glued to both; the board overhangs past it toward the pickup. The tenon passes down through a notch in the top, so the neck is set from above after the body is closed.
- **Neck:** set neck at 5 degrees with a mortise and tenon. The tenon sits below the top, and the neck clears the body at about fret 15. '59 roundback profile, 12" radius, 22 frets. 13-degree 3x3 Juris headstock: sides flaring straight from the nut width (no wings), a pointed gable top, flat face with square edges for standard tuners, ebony faceplate, pointed nickel truss rod cover, posts following the flare. A lofted volute (about 4.5 mm proud) blends the neck into the headstock behind the nut.
- **Electronics:** no pickguard. An EMG 91 active floating humbucker hangs from a strap under the fingerboard extension; its lead goes into the body through a 6 mm hole in the top hidden under the board. A stacked volume/tone pot, the stereo jack and a battery door share a 100 × 44 × 2 mm plate on the flat treble lower-bout face of the rim, covering an 84 × 30 mm window. The 9 V slides end-first into a sleeve behind the door, so it changes without removing the plate; the active pickup needs no string ground. The window is a notch cut from the split face of each rim half, and the wall behind the plate is left the full 19 mm ledge thickness for the screws.
