    Construction

    One-piece rim routed from 12/4 Honduran mahogany, 2.25 in finished for a 2.5 in body: 7 mm walls, 12 × 8 mm glue ledges routed in place of kerfed linings, solid neck and tail blocks, mortise for a set neck. Spruce top carved as flat planes: a 5 mm hexagonal bridge plateau inside a symmetric eight-sided arch zone, 14 facets in all, with a flat skirt outside it that lands on the rim. A 5 × 6 mm ring beam and two 6 × 9 mm parallel tone bars with scalloped ends run under the plateau's long sides, and a 6 × 7 mm cross bar braces the flat upper bout from the neck block. Back is a shallower faceted arch, glued on.

    The pickguard never touches the top. It carries the pickup on its tab and is held at two points: a strap screwed into the treble rim, and a 1.5 mm plate under the fingerboard extension, where the guard and the board's underside share one plane.
