The C-75, plainly

The one now allocating
PriceUSD 3,500
AvailabilityFive this year, built to order. Requisitions are taken by waitlist now; serial numbers will follow its order.
Lead timeAbout six months from deposit: 90 days for the body and neck to be made, then 12 weeks of finishing and assembly in Portland.An estimate, given in writing when your build starts.
BodyMahogany, 2.0" deep, with a 0.2" maple cap. One U-shaped chamber through both wings and across the lower bout; a solid centre block carries the pickups and bridge.
Edges5/8" flat bevels on the top and back, leaving a 3/4" band round the side. Where the top bevel cuts through the cap, the maple shows as a natural line round the edge.
Size18.00" long · 13.49" across the lower bout
NeckMahogany, set and glued at 4°. Meets the body at the 16th fret.
Neck carve’59 roundback, shared by all three models. 0.86" deep at the 1st fret, 0.96" at the 12th.
NutGraph Tech, 1-11/16" (42.9 mm) wide.
FingerboardEbony, 12" radius.
Frets22, nickel silver, 6105 wire (0.090" wide, 0.055" tall).
Scale24.75"
Headstock3+3, tilted back 11.2°. Truss rod adjusted through a slot above the nut, under a rosewood cover. Riveted asset tag.
TunersGrover Deluxe 133N, nickel, three per side.
PickupsTV Jones T-Armond, two, English mount.The prototype carries no-ear pickups; production instruments get English-mount T-Armonds.
BridgeBrass Compton, one-piece and compensated, on brass thumbwheels over a rosewood base.
TailpieceAllparts short trapeze.
VibratoNot offered. The C-75 is designed around the trapeze and the Compton bridge, not a vibrato.
ControlsVolume and tone, amber speck ribbed knobs. Full-size three-way tone switch between them; pickup selector on the bass horn. Both switches under skirted dress nuts.
JackElectrosocket, treble side.
FinishShellac, then oil and wax, rubbed by hand. No lacquer.Shellac is a soft, repairable finish. It wears where your arm rests; that's expected, and it can be refreshed.
ColoursWalnut stain over the maple cap (as built) or natural maple. Solid colours in milk paint: black, aged computer white, primer grey.
Weight6.55 lb, calculated from the model. Your guitar is weighed on a scale before the balance is due.
Strings and setupStringjoy Flyers, .012 set with a plain 3rd, as on the builder’s own. Action 2.0 mm (5/64") at the 12th fret.
CaseUnder review.
